The competition that inspires us

What is FIRST?

FIRST, which means “For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology,” is a non-profit organization for high school students. The intent of FIRST is “to create a world where science and technology are celebrated… where young people dream of becoming science and technology heroes…” -Dean Kamen (Founder of FIRST).
