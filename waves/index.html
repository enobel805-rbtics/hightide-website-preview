Introducing Ripcurrent.

Our 2026 robot, and the world champion on Einstein in Houston. Students designed, machined, wired and programmed all of it.

This is the team's own CAD, drawn live. Drag to turn it, and keep scrolling to take it apart.

  1. Shooter

    Four Kraken X44s and .25″ copper flywheels that store energy for every shot.

  2. Turret

    55.7:1 on one Kraken X44, with a flex-wheel cable-chain tensioner.

  3. Dye Rotor

    A 36.4:1 rotation reduction.

  4. Intake

    84+ balls under the trench, 100+ over the bump.

  5. Bumpers

    Structural. They double as hopper walls.

  6. Drivetrain

    A 25″ × 32″ swerve base, geared 7.67:1 for low current draw.
